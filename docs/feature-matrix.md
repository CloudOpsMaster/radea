# Article coverage matrix

This matrix maps all 80 articles to practical RADEA scenarios and their limits.
Articles 1–20 are in `articles/radea` and articles 21–80 are in `articles`.
The product distribution excludes the article corpus. The
[rule reference](rules.md) traces deterministic rules to source articles.

- **Covered** indicates a verified scenario within the stated scope.
- **Partial** indicates a scenario with an outstanding implementation step.
- **Aligned** indicates an architectural or team principle.
- **Out of scope** indicates a topic without an implementation.

Summary: covered — 80, partial — 0, aligned — 0, out of scope — 0.
